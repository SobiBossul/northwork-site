Northwork, once known as Qwerly, began with a simple idea: create a place where game concepts could grow into something real. Before Qwerly, there was another community called Nexhub. Nexhub was eventually discontinued, and on December 29th, 2025, Qwerly was born. Founded by Sobi, it started as a Roblox development community built around simple game projects, before gradually growing into a broader creative vision that reaches beyond Roblox. A few days after Qwerly was created, RAFA_SPECTRE joined the community as co-founder, becoming part of its journey from its early days.

On February 8th, 2026, Qwerly collaborated with M.C. Murkoff Corporation, the game development community founded by RAFA_SPECTRE. During the collaboration, Qwerly held up to 95% ownership and 95% contribution. On May 14th, 2026, Qwerly Romania was introduced, focusing on games inspired by Romania, Sobi’s home country. Then, on June 7th, 2026, Qwerly collaborated with Magic SMP and RyTexhNetwork.

On July 3rd, 2026, Qwerly became Northwork, introducing a new name, a new logo, and a renewed direction. Northwork Experiments followed on July 8th, 2026, focusing on lighter, less demanding development projects. On August 19th, 2026, Northwork Science was introduced, dedicated to UI-focused games and experiences.

Today, many of our games are still ideas and concepts, but we believe they can become real experiences soon. Progress can be gradual because Sobi is currently the only person actively building and developing projects. Although RAFA_SPECTRE joined as co-founder a few days after Qwerly was created, day-to-day development is still mainly handled by Sobi. Northwork is still growing, and every idea is another possibility waiting to become reality.
